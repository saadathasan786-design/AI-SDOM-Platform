# Repository Register — Architecture Decision Records

This directory is the Repository Register for Architecture Decision Records
(ADRs), per [AI-SDOM-STD-0006 (Section 9)] and [AI-SDOM-PRC-0002
(Section 10.2)].

## Identifier Authority

ADR identifiers are drawn from this register. They SHALL fall within the ADR
reserved range 0001-9999 per [AI-SDOM-ARC-0001 (Section 12)] and SHALL NOT be
invented; AI assistance SHALL NOT generate identifiers per [AI-SDOM-ARC-0001
(Section 14.4)].
